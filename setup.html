3Where is the station?

Easiest way: open Google Maps, right-click your house, and click the numbers at the top of the menu that pops up. That copies them; paste them here. They're used for sunrise, sunset and the forecast.

4Tell me when it stops

If readings stop coming in for a while, the station sends you a message, and another when they're back. The simplest way is the free ntfy app on your phone:

1. Install ntfy from the App Store or Google Play.
2. In the app, press + and subscribe to the topic name below (it's random so nobody else gets your messages).
3. Press Send a test.

Or by email

For Gmail, make an app password (Google account → Security → App passwords) and use it here, not your normal password.

And if the whole Pi goes off

Nothing on the Pi can tell you it lost power. A free service like healthchecks.io can: make a check there with a 10-minute period, and paste its ping address here. The station pings it every few minutes; when the pings stop, the service emails you.

Saving restarts the station, which takes a few seconds. Then the dashboard shows each sensor as its next reading comes in (the hub sends every five minutes).

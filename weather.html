can't log in?

Inside this pane, the site's login cookie counts as a third-party cookie, and the browser may block it. Allow it once for this dashboard and logging in here works:

  • Chrome / Edge: click the eye icon at the right of the address bar and allow third-party cookies for this site. Or: Settings → Privacy and security → Third-party cookies → Allowed to use third-party cookies → Add, and enter this page's address.
  • Firefox: click the shield icon left of the address bar and turn off Enhanced Tracking Protection for this site.
  • Safari: Settings → Privacy → untick Prevent cross-site tracking (this applies to every site).

Each address you open the dashboard from counts as its own site. Or use “open ↗” to view the page in its own tab.

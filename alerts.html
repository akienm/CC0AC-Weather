Alerts ← DashboardSetup (where alerts go)

Each row is one alert: what it watches, when it starts, how long it stays on, and where it goes. Messages go to the ntfy topic and email address on the setup page; dashboard shows it as a banner there. Saved rows take effect at the next check, within a minute.

These rows are the ones your earlier settings meant. Nothing changes until you save.

AlertKindWatchingTriggerStays onFlagsTarget

Watching
any, or one sensor's name or id (for a receiver: radio, hub or console).
Trigger
The line it crosses, in miles, °F, inHg or minutes whatever the dashboard shows; for an NWS alert: any, minor, moderate, severe or extreme (that or worse).
Stays on
until clears: while it is so. hh:mm:ss (or minutes): that long after the last time it was so (lightning: after the last close strike). once: a message each time, never "over".
Flags
new: a row on its own. replaces: rows of one kind take turns: one message when the first starts, one when a stricter one starts ("closer"), one "over" when the last ends. over: say when it ends. repeat:hh:mm:ss: say it again that often while it stays on. off: kept, but does nothing.
Target
all, or any of ntfy, email, dashboard.

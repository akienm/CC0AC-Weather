# CC0AC Weather — privacy policy

*Applies to the CC0AC Weather Android app (`org.cc0ac.weatherkiosk`) and to the
station software in this repository. Last changed November 10, 2026.*

**The app collects nothing about you, and sends nothing to us or anyone else.**

- **What it connects to:** only the weather station address *you* type in. It
  reads that station's current conditions and forecast, the same files your own
  browser would read from it. It connects to no other server.
- **What it stores:** the station address and your display settings, on the phone
  itself. Nothing leaves the phone. Uninstalling the app deletes them.
- **No accounts, analytics, ads, tracking, or crash reporting.** The app contains
  no third-party code that does any of these.
- **Permissions:** *Internet* and *network state*, to reach your station;
  *run at startup*, so the widget and a wall display come back after the phone
  restarts.
- **Kiosk mode** can ask to become the phone's home screen and to pin itself to
  the screen. Android asks you first, and pressing Back leaves it.

The station software runs on your own computer and keeps your weather data
there. If you also send readings to services such as Weather Underground or
AcuRite, those services' own privacy policies apply to what they receive.

Questions: open an issue at <https://github.com/akienm/CC0AC-Weather/issues>.
